Community Guidelines

DateToday · Version 2026-10-17.v1 · Contact: support@datetoday.app

Plain-language rules for Dating, Friend Date, and every plan on DateToday. Full in-app text: Settings → Community Guidelines.

Respect other people

Treat people like humans you might meet in person. Respect someone’s boundaries, time, and “no.”

No harassment or threats

No harassment, stalking, threats, intimidation, or violence — in the app or around meetings arranged through DateToday. Unwanted repeated contact after someone passes, unmatches, blocks, or stops talking is not okay.

No hate or discrimination

No hate speech or discrimination based on identity, orientation, race, religion, disability, gender, or similar.

No unwanted sexual behavior

No sexual harassment, sexual pressure, or non-consensual sexual content. Keep photos and videos appropriate for an app you would open in public.

Be real — no fake identities

Use your own photos and videos. No impersonation, catfishing, stolen media, or fake profiles. Optional verification is not a background check.

No scams or fraud

No scams, solicitation, investment pitches, spam, coercion, or moving people off-platform for fraud.

Respect a no

If someone passes, unmatches, blocks, cancels a plan, or ends communication, stop. Do not pressure anyone to meet or continue talking.

Protect privacy

Do not share another person’s private information, photos, or messages without permission. Do not expose someone’s home, work, or other precise location.

18+ only

Minors are not allowed — as users or in content. Report suspected underage accounts immediately.

Dating and Friend Date

The same conduct and safety rules apply to Dating, Friend Date, and other plans. Platonic intent does not make harassment or pressure okay.

Meetups

Meet only when both people agree. Prefer public places for first meetups, tell a friend, arrange your own transportation, and leave if you feel unsafe.

Enforcement

Serious or repeated violations can lead to content removal, feature limits, suspension, or permanent removal. We review reports when we can, but we do not promise a specific outcome for every report.

Reporting

Block and report in the app (Safety Center). Report problems by email: support@datetoday.app.
