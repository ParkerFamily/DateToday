Privacy Policy

DateToday · Version 2026-09-17.v1 · Contact: support@datetoday.app

Who we are

DateToday helps people go live, discover nearby matches for tonight, and plan real dates. This policy describes what we collect in the current product. Firebase is our primary backend.

What we collect

  • Account data: email, auth provider identifiers, date of birth (age gate).
  • Profile: photos, videos, display name, bio, dating preferences, discovery filters.
  • Live / Ping session state while you are available tonight.
  • Messages and date-plan content you create with matches.
  • Safety signals: blocks, reports, and related metadata.
  • Optional identity verification data processed by Persona (not stored as raw ID images in our Storage by design).
  • Purchase / subscription status from Apple, Google, and RevenueCat when you buy DateToday+ or Tonight Boost.
  • Approximate device / app diagnostics needed to run the service. Exact coordinates are not written to Firestore today.

How we use data

To operate accounts, discovery, Live/Ping, messaging, safety tools, verification, and paid features; to prevent abuse; and to comply with law. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share data with processors that help run the app (Firebase/Google, Apple/Google billing, RevenueCat, Persona for optional verification, and email/support tools). Public profile fields are visible to other signed-in users as needed for dating discovery.

Retention & deletion

You can request account deletion in the app (Settings → Delete account) or by emailing support@datetoday.app. Some records may be retained as required for safety, fraud, or legal obligations.

Children

DateToday is 18+. We do not knowingly allow minors.
