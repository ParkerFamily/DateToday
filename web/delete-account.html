Delete your DateToday account

Google Play requires a web path to request account deletion. You can delete entirely in the DateToday app, or request deletion here.

In-app (recommended)

  1. Open DateToday and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account & Security → Delete account.
  3. Read the deletion notice, manage any App Store / Play subscriptions, re-authenticate, and confirm.

Deleting your DateToday account does not automatically cancel Apple or Google subscriptions. Manage those in your store account settings.

Request by email

Email support@datetoday.app from the email on your DateToday account with subject Account deletion request. Include your account email and that you want permanent deletion.

We will verify ownership and process deletion of account data that is not legally required to retain for fraud, safety, chargeback, or regulatory purposes.
